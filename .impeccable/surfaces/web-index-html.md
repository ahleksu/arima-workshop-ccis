# Surface brief: web explorer

Scope: the whole web explorer (Home, Lecture, Hands-on, Demos, Glossary). Visitor mode: Read, with a Lecture page that is presented on a projector.

Audience, job: CCIS faculty who learn ARIMA in one hour (20 minutes of lecture, 40 minutes of hands-on in Colab). The presenter drives the lecture with arrow keys.

Action: every page ends in one visible next step (next beat, open the notebook, next block).

Content: seven beats, three notebook blocks, three demos, 29 glossary terms. All real. The series is synthetic and labeled so.

Constraints: light mode only, static site, no network at runtime, bundled fonts, WCAG AA, 390 px to 1440 px.

Chosen direction: line map (assigned). Memorable moment: the hour drawn as two transit lines that meet at one interchange at minute 20.

Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The hour is a journey on two lines, and every screen is a station sign. It refuses the slide-deck and docs-sidebar arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: White enamel signs on a pale platform ground. Near-black ink. Four named line colors: Lecture blue, Hands-on orange, Demos green, Reference plum. Barlow Semi Condensed for signs and tabular minutes, Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading. Round stops on a thick line. Hairline rules, small radii, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees where they are in the hour, understands one idea per stop, and always knows the next stop. They believe the plan fits in 60 minutes, and they open the next notebook or beat.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home. Left: the title in 5rem sign type with date and time. Below it the two-line map across the full width, with the interchange at minute 20. A tall orange button "Start the lecture" sits under the Lecture line. The clock sits in the header, right.

FORM: Transit line diagram with station signage. Position 3 of my list (after strip-chart recorder and statistics monograph). Seed key 80c26789.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
